Basis and limits

  • Source: AW139 RFM Rev 30, Section 4, Fig. 4-43 (up to 6400 kg, page 4-65) and Supplement 50, Fig. 4-37 (6400 to 6800 kg, page S50-63). Curves extracted from the vector PDF.
  • The chart does not include clearance. Flyaway capability exists when hover height ≥ height loss + clearance (15 ft / 5 m minimum).
  • Headwind credit is already factored 50% in the chart. Valid for 100% or 102% NR, with the flyaway initiated within 1 second of engine failure recognition.
  • If the weight at engine failure is at or below the Hover OGE 2.5 min OEI weight, an engine failure in hover gives no height loss, provided the pilot does not intervene on the controls (RFM note).
  • Above 6800 kg there is no flyaway chart in the RFM Rev 30 used here. Weights below 4400 kg use the 4400 kg curve (conservative).
  • Support tool. The approved RFM prevails.
